# Genome of the Twin Kings

## Overview

Genome of the Twin Kings is a bioinformatics-themed educational RPG that combines:

- Dark fantasy storytelling
- Bioinformatics concepts
- Coding puzzles
- Mystery and forensic investigation
- Resident Evil-inspired atmosphere
- 3D cinematic introduction
- 2D gameplay for scalability

The game teaches real bioinformatics workflows through exploration, puzzles, dungeons, and evidence-based problem solving.

---

## Story

Two identical twins exist within the Kingdom of Helix:

- Aren (Hero)
- Varen (Villain)

After a series of adventures, the villain successfully impersonates the hero and convinces the kingdom that he defeated the true criminal.

The villain marries Princess Lyra, seizes power, and slowly turns the kingdom into a tyrannical state built on misinformation and manipulated evidence.

Meanwhile, the real hero is imprisoned inside a series of biological dungeons.

Using intelligence, coding skills, and scientific reasoning, the hero escapes, gathers evidence, exposes the fraud, and restores truth to the kingdom.

The final victory is achieved through proof rather than violence.

## Coding Puzzle System

Players interact with ancient bioinformatics terminals.

Example categories:

### Sequence Alignment

Find mutations between sequences.

### FASTQ Cleanup

Filter low-quality sequencing reads.

### BLAST Challenges

Identify organisms from sequence similarity.

### Protein Analysis

Determine biochemical properties from amino acid sequences.

### Debugging Challenges

Repair broken Python code.

Reward types:

- XP
- Data Fragments
- Dungeon Keys
- Scientific Evidence

---

## Enemy Design

### Batch Effect Phantom

Represents experimental noise.

Weakness:

- Proper normalization

### PCR Chimera

Creates duplicate copies of itself.

Weakness:

- Alignment analysis

### Sequencing Wraith

Generates misleading reads.

Weakness:

- Quality filtering

### Variant Dragon

Boss creature.

Weakness:

- Variant interpretation

---

## Villain Arc

The villain manipulates:

- Research reports
- Experimental results
- Historical records
- Genomic evidence

Players uncover the fraud through investigation and analysis.

---

## Final Trial

The final boss encounter is not a combat sequence.

Players must:

1. Load evidence
2. Analyze datasets
3. Compare genomic profiles
4. Build a scientific case
5. Present findings to villagers

Possible outcomes:

- False evidence → Case dismissed
- Strong evidence → Villain exposed
