GENOME OF THE TWIN KINGS — PROLOGUE & FIVE CHAPTERS

This is a Godot 4.4.1 Web export. Start the included local HTTP server in this folder:

  python3 serve.py

Then open http://localhost:8000 in a modern WebGL 2-capable browser. A standard `python3 -m http.server 8000` also works. Do not open index.html directly with file://. The first load includes the Godot engine and may take a moment.

A/D or arrow keys move; E or Space interacts. Click diamond markers to walk to objects. J opens the evidence journal; Escape pauses. On-screen movement and interaction buttons are also available. For best readability, use a desktop or landscape screen.

Complete the dungeon comparison and enter Sequence Forest. Classify the sprite's examples to open the forest arch. At Alignment River's ledger, choose all four reads in order with at least three matching bases at every join; restore the boat and approach the eastern landing. In Expression Swamp, listen to the Kraken and inspect the stone-table assay. Choose a control and a case that both pass the supplied QC check and share a batch, then select their counts per million (CPM) in control/case order. Reach the marsh arch. In the Dungeon of Missing Data, speak with Draco and inspect the ledger: filter by the displayed quality cutoff, classify each site and choose the candidate's alternate and passing-read support. Then approach the archive door.

In the Hall of Records, talk to Princess Lyra and visit the evidence table. Select statements supported by the five fragments, compare the records and choose a defensible conclusion and next step. Use Open Journal at the table to check sources and limitations without losing current selections. Unsupported claims are returned for revision. After preparing the case, bring it to the lectern for the public hearing. Preparing the case earns a one-time sixth evidence entry; all rewards total 1,050 XP. Draft selections reset if the table is closed or the game reloaded before the case is prepared. You can revisit a region at its completion screen, but backtracking after moving onward is not yet implemented.

The Forest examples are simplified and pre-aligned, not a variant-calling pipeline. River uses exact overlaps of error-free, oriented reads and is not a real genome assembler. The Swamp's QC labels are provided, not calculated, and CPM is a basic library-size scaling exercise, not differential-expression analysis. One sample per condition cannot establish equality or significance; CPM does not remove batch effects. The dragon's ledger uses supplied alignments and a toy quality/depth rule; low coverage is a no-call, not a reference match. A candidate is not a confirmed variant or diagnosis. Real calls require additional mapping, duplicate, strand and independent checks.

The five fragments are clues with separate sources, not proof of identity or tampering. The Final Trial combines the fictional prison record with an additional reread of the same aliquot and a fictional public summary. The profile/summary discrepancy does not identify a twin or prove intent; a same-aliquot reread may share errors. The hearing introduces an additional authenticated office order and Varen's admission to explain the outcome; the short profiles alone do not establish his role. Preserve original records, audit custody and seek independent review.

Progress is stored in the browser's local IndexedDB for the site origin and can be lost if site data is cleared or storage is blocked. Valid saves from previous builds migrate automatically when served from the same origin and no newer save exists. A completed Dungeon of Missing Data save begins at the Final Trial; a completed Swamp save begins at the Dungeon; a completed River save begins at the Swamp; a completed Forest save begins at the River; a completed prologue-only save begins at the Forest. Unfinished journeys stay in their existing regions. Older saves are left intact. Changing host or port changes the origin. If the browser still shows an older cached version after upgrading, hard-refresh without clearing site data. A corrupt newer save does not silently fall back to an older one. Starting over replaces the active save after confirmation. No cloud save is included.

This illustrated 2D prototype includes the prologue and five chapters through a scripted story ending. Backtracking, hostile encounters and bosses, the envisioned 3D cinematic, audio and the Python sandbox are not built. Guides and other characters use scripted dialogue; the encounters are not combat.
